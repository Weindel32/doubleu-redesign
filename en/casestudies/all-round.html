Italy Tennis Padel 2024–2026

All Round
Sport & Wellness

Rome - Italy

The Club

All Round Sport & Wellness is a leading sports centre in Rome, with a structured Tennis School and Padel School, programmes for every level and a qualified technical staff. A sporting environment that demands identity, visual coherence and materials up to the task.

The Challenge
01

Unifying the image of the two schools — tennis and padel — into a coherent visual system.

02

Providing staff and coaches with an exclusive kit that communicates professionalism and belonging.

03

Simplifying order management with direct, reserved access for club members.

04

Creating a distinct visual identity for the Padel School, a rapidly growing discipline.

05

Setting up an exhibition space within the club for the personalised DOUBLEU line.

06

Finding a reliable partner for events, tournaments and promotional activities on the territory.

The DOUBLEU Solution
Essential Line

Tennis School

Technical t-shirt, shorts and skirt, sweatshirt. White/black palette, lightweight and breathable materials. Minimal design with embroidered club logo.

Premium Line

Staff, Padel & Competition

Navy/beige/bordeaux palette for the Padel School. Cream white/grey for staff. Ergonomic fit, premium fabrics, complete tracksuit kit and competition uniform.

Digital

Members' Private Area

Dedicated e-commerce accessible exclusively to club members. Direct purchase of the personalised line, centralised orders, tailor-made experience.

Additional Services
Exhibition Space

Setting up a dedicated space within the club to display and sell the personalised DOUBLEU line.

Tournament Sponsorship

Presence as official sponsor at club tournaments and demo days. Shared visibility, co-marketing on the territory.

Summer Camp Supplier

Official supplier for the club's summer camps. Dedicated kit for young athletes, centralised order management.

The Results
✓

Strong and recognisable visual identity

Both schools speak a single visual language, recognisable on and off the court.

✓

Performance and quality without compromise

Premium technical materials tailored to the needs of every category — from school to competitive teams.

✓

Exclusive purchasing experience

Members access a dedicated platform: the club and its identity become a tangible privilege.

✓

Management efficiency

Centralised orders, planned deliveries, a single point of contact. Zero operational dispersion.
