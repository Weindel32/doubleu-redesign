International Tennis Since 2019

SNAUWAERT

International heritage. Made in Italy product development.

Nearly a century of tennis history transformed into a contemporary apparel collection.

The Brand

Founded in 1928, Snauwaert is one of the most iconic brands in the history of world tennis. When the brand decided to strengthen its presence in the apparel segment, the choice fell on an Italian partner capable of transforming values, identity and sporting tradition into a contemporary collection.

Since 2019, DOUBLEU has been collaborating with Snauwaert on the development of dedicated apparel collections, contributing to the design, prototyping and production of men's and women's garments for the international market.

For Snauwaert it was essential to find a partner who could concretely express the values of Made in Italy: manufacturing quality, attention to detail, production flexibility and expertise in product development.

The Challenge

The Process

The project took shape through a complete product development journey: from the first creative studies and technical sketches to the realisation of prototypes, through to the production of collections used by Snauwaert in their communication and promotional activities.

The goal was to create an apparel line consistent with the brand's historic heritage, maintaining a contemporary aesthetic language and a strong, recognisable identity.
