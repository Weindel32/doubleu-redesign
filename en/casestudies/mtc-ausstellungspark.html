The Club

MTC Ausstellungspark is one of the most dynamic tennis clubs in the Munich scene. A community-driven club that brings together people of all ages and backgrounds around a passion for tennis, with a strong focus on youth development, competitive men's, women's and junior teams, and an intense club life.

A sought-after club, with a waiting list for new members — a sign of a strong identity and a solid community. This is the context in which the partnership with DOUBLEU was born, launched at the end of 2025.

The Challenge
01

Launching MTC's first Club-Shop, finding a partner who shared the club's values — not just a product supplier.

02

Expressing the club's identity through an exclusive and individual design, recognisable by the community.

03

Offering members Made in Italy quality — premium technical materials, not standard club apparel.

04

Integrating tennis into members' daily lifestyle, from the court to the city.

05

Managing order logistics in a simple, centralised and sustainable way — without burdening club staff.

The DOUBLEU Solution
Exclusive Collection

MTC Club Line

Line designed exclusively for MTC in the club's design. Match jerseys for U18, men and women, cap and shorts. Made in Italy technical materials — breathable, lightweight, elastic, with moisture management, thermal protection and antibacterial treatment.

Digital

MTC's first Club-Shop

Dedicated e-commerce for the club at doubleutennis.com/eshop-mtc-ausstellungspark — every member orders the MTC-design collection directly. Continuously expanding assortment, managed together with the club.

Visit the Club Shop
Logistics

Collective Ordering System

Collective ordering system — order collection by a single deadline, centralised delivery to the clubhouse. Reduced shipping costs, lowered environmental impact, simple management for the club.

The Launch

A party,
a collection,
a community.

At the club's Christmas party, all MTC youth members received the new exclusive DOUBLEU shirts as a gift. A launch that united brand and community from day one — making the shirts not just a product, but a shared moment.

“
Thanks to our collaboration with DOUBLEU and our MTC Club Line, we were able to help our members take to the court as a unified team, wearing the identity and colours of the club.

Our youth benefit from the strengthened team spirit and feel strongly connected to the club.

The adult teams particularly appreciate the high quality of the materials, the attention to detail, and the consideration of individual design preferences.

Friendly, approachable and Made in Italy.

Fabian Arnold
Head of Sports, MTC Ausstellungspark – Munich
