About us

DOUBLEU is an Italian brand specialising in the design and production of premium custom sportswear for tennis, padel and pickleball clubs. From match uniforms and school kits to off-court collections and dedicated member shops, we build every project around the club's identity, never starting from a standard catalogue.

Identity

We shape your club's heritage and transform it into a distinctive and coherent visual identity.

Design

Timeless design, attention to detail and coordinated collections for every area of your club.

Production
